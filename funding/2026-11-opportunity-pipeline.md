## Prioritization
1. Commercial paid work and verified fixed-price bounties with clear acceptance criteria.
2. Google Patch Rewards: find an eligible project and obtain maintainer agreement before writing a substantial patch.
3. Security bounty programs: only submit verified, in-scope, non-duplicate findings.
4. Grants/credits: submit accurate project evidence; do not count credits as revenue.

## Controls
- No credential use, exploitation of third-party accounts, or scanning outside program scope.
- No mass speculative or AI-generated vulnerability submissions.
- Verify geographic eligibility, identity requirements, payment method, and tax terms before submission.
- Track submission ID, decision, amount awarded, and amount actually received separately.
