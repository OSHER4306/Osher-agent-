# Enterprise Fixer — 10,000 company outreach operating plan

**Target:** 10,000 unique, qualified companies in the initial prospect universe. This is a target, not a claim of 10,000 contacts reached.

## Pipeline definitions

- **Identified:** company with official domain and source URL
- **Verified:** identity, market, public business contact route and dated evidence of a relevant need checked
- **Qualified:** specific problem hypothesis and service match (Audit, Sprint, Enterprise)
- **Contacted:** individualized communication actually sent through a permitted channel; retain timestamp, channel and delivery ID
- **Replied / Meeting / Proposal / Contract / Collected:** record only when evidence exists
- **Suppressed:** opt-out, refusal, invalid address, duplicate, or other no-contact reason

## Outreach guardrails

- Do not scrape personal emails or infer private contact information.
- Use official business channels and honor local marketing and privacy rules.
- Personalize based on verified facts; avoid fabricated urgency or guaranteed ROI.
- Respect opt-outs, rate limits, and prior contact history across all automations.
- Do not count drafts as sent or accepted proposals as collected cash.
- Check capacity and execution partners before offering deliverables.

## Commercial positioning

**Fixer Audit:** paid problem diagnosis and reproducible evidence.
**Fixer Sprint:** scoped proof-of-value and tested implementation.
**Enterprise Fixer:** milestone-based larger engineering engagements.

Suggested pricing bands are hypotheses and require qualification, scoping and written acceptance.
